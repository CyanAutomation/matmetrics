# 2026-08-20 - Judo Session: Randori

## Techniques Practiced
- O\-soto\-gari
- Juji\-gatame
- Sankaku\-jime
- Yoko\-tomoe\-nage
- Ashi\-guruma

## Session Description

We started off with the normal warm up, working through breakfalls, rolls, and hiki\-dashi drills\. After that, we moved into three rounds of ne\-waza, where I was able to chain a failed juji\-gatame into a sankaku\-jime\. Then we did multiple rounds of tachi\-waza randori, where I made a conscious effort to focus on grip fighting, especially using the pistol grip, which worked out well\. Capitalising on this control, I managed to successfully execute a yoko\-tomoe\-nage, an ashi\-guruma, and multiple good throws of o\-soto\-gari\.

## Notes

It was a good randori, where I went into it with a specific goal in mind: working on kumi\-kata\. This paid off, as I felt much more in control, overall\.
