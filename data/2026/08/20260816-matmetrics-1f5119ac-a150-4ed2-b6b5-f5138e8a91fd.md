# 2026-08-16 - Judo Session: Shiai

## Techniques Practiced
- Ko\-soto\-gake
- O\-uchi\-gari
- Ko\-soto\-gari

## Session Description

Today was the Dan Grading event at AWE Aldermaston\. My first match was against Mr Golden Kiwi, someone I've fought before\. I managed to score a waza\-ari with my ko\-soto\-gake, which actually scored as an ippon in a previous match against him\. He then equalized with a waza\-ari of his own, making it a tie\. Despite having a few close scoring opportunities as time ran out, the match ended in a draw\. The next couple of matches didn't go so well\. In the second, I tried a new drop o\-uchi\-gari; it felt good for pressure but didn't translate into a score\. The third match ended with me losing to an ude\-garami attack, which was frustrating\. My fourth match was against Mr Honeydew, a younger second dan from Pinewood\. He had a really quick pace and strong gripping, but I was able to find an opening, scoring a waza\-ari – I think it was a nidan ko\-soto\-gari\. I managed to stay ahead for the rest of the match and win that one\.

## Notes

Today was tough, mentally\. I felt a high amount of anxiety and pressure prior travelling to the Dan grading event, as well as at the event\. I should have won my first match \- there were some close opportunities that I should've worked harder on\. Another judoka said that the mistake that I made was "slowing to match his pace", instead of working at my pace\. I also had my elbow hurt in the ude\-garami, which I don't feel good about\. Overall, I got 5 points out of it, but I don't feel very positive or fulfilled by the event today\.
