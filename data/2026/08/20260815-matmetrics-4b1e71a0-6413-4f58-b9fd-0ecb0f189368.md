# 2026-08-15 - Judo Session: Technical

## Techniques Practiced
- Ushiro\-ukemi
- Sankaku\-jime
- Anaconda choke
- Darce choke
- Buggy choke

## Session Description

Started today's session with a cardio\-focused warm\-up, incorporating burpees, back breakfalls, and some bear walks\. We then moved into the technical portion, which was a no\-gi session\. The first technique we drilled was the anaconda choke, which felt pretty good and worked well for me\. We then moved onto the Darce choke; I found that one a bit more complex to execute smoothly\. Next, we practiced a standard triangle choke, which went fairly well\. Lastly, we tried the buggy choke, but that particular submission definitely didn't click for me today\. We finished off the session with two three\-minute rounds of ne\-waza no\-gi randori\.

## Notes

It was a nice to have a change of focus and do something different with a no\-gi session\. It causes you to think differently \- as there is no lapel or sleeve to grab\. The darce and buggy choke were not very practical \- but it felt good to do the no\-gi randori\.
