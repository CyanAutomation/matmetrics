# 2026-09-24 - Judo Session: Technical

## Techniques Practiced
- Juji\-gatame

## Session Description

We started off with a dynamic stretching warm\-up, then straight into drilling pulling guard, which is like a failed tomoe\-nage\. Then we went into scythe armbars, juji\-gatame, from the near\-side, and then a step\-over variation from the opponent escaping knee\-on\-belly\. Finally, we practiced escapes from knee\-on\-belly, including pushing the knee backwards and scooping the nearside leg, and rotating away\.
