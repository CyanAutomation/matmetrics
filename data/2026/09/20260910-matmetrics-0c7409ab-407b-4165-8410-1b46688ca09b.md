# 2026-09-10 - Judo Session: Randori

## Techniques Practiced
- O\-soto\-gari
- Uchi\-mata

## Session Description

After a normal Thursday warmup down the mat, we started with two rounds of ne\-waza randori\. Then we started the tough session, first starting with four rounds of randori and a short break, repeated thrice\. I worked on my grip fighting, battling other lefties and my o\-soto\-gari too\. The o\-soto\-gari is developing well, and I was able to successfully get a very clean one during my opponent standing tall and trying to break grips\. I made sure to try out the Satoshi Ishii shoulder pocket\-grip, which seems to work well as an interim grip\. A small highlight, but I was able to hit a jumping uchi\-mata against a beginner that was executed perfectly\. It was a very tough session\.
