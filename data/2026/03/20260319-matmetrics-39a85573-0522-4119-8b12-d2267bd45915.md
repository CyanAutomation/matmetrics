# 2026-03-19 - Judo Session: Randori

## Techniques Practiced
- Mae\-ukemi
- Ushiro\-ukemi
- Uchi\-mata
- Ko\-soto\-gari
- Ko\-uchi\-gari

## Session Description

The session began with a standard five\-minute warm\-up, including running and practicing Mae\-ukemi and Ushiro\-ukemi\. Following this, we moved into ten minutes of Uchi\-komi, which was paired with some turn\-over drills; it felt quite demanding\. The main part of the session was an intense, non\-stop Tachi\-waza Randori\. During Randori, I focused on my grip fighting, as well as attempting Uchi\-mata and Ko\-soto\-gari\. I was particularly pleased to land a strong Uchi\-mata into Ko\-uchi\-gari combination, which is something I've consistently found challenging\. My key takeaway was realizing the timing for Ko\-uchi\-gari in that sequence\. Instead of using it too early or just as a prop behind the ankle, I found it most effective when applied precisely as Uke settled their weight back\. This allowed their front foot to become light, turning the Ko\-uchi\-gari into a true cutting sweep rather than a block\. We concluded the session with five minutes of Newaza Randori\.

## Notes

At one point, I felt so gassed I thought I was going to vomit\. But, on the positive side, I got some good rounds with some left handers\.
