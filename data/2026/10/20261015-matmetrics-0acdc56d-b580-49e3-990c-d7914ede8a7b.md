# 2026-10-15 - Judo Session: Technical

## Techniques Practiced
- Kata\-juji\-jime

## Session Description

We started off the BJJ session with just a guard\-passing warm\-up, which is a nice way to warm up\. Then we went into cross chokes, specifically kata juji jime, from the bottom in closed guard\. We started with the normal technique using a lapel or shoulder grab, then changed it to grab the defending hand and push it down towards the pocket\. Then we did a nice variation where from the hug, we slipped the thumb behind the lapel and looped around before doing the cross choke\. I was paired with two white belts, which made it a light and easy session, with a focus on technique\.

## Notes

It has been a nice change of pace in doing these lighter and more technical sessions\.
