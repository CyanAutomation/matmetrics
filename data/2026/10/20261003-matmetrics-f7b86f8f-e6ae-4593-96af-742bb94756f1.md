# 2026-10-03 - Judo Session: Randori

## Techniques Practiced
- Sode\-tsuri\-komi\-harai

## Session Description

Today we started with ten minutes of stations, rotating between high\-knees, star jumps, mountain climbers, planks, and ukemi\. Once that was done, we went into light randori where first point wins\. I took it light and went against three opponents, letting my third opponent, a junior belt, throw me before I retired to the sideline\. We then went into three rounds of ne\-waza randori, where I chose some more junior opponents due to my injury and worked on being fluid with transitions\. I was pleased to see I was able to successfully pull off a shoulder shrug from a turtle under\-hook\. We then went into full randori, where I partnered with an impressive junior belt who was my weight\. I was alternating between lefty and righty, and at one point, I was able to carry out a lapel grip break into a sode\-tsuri\-komi\-harai, that was impressive\.

## Notes

It was my first randori since my elbow injury, so I was quite anxious about it\. I found that taking my standard left\-hand grip was not great as it opened my elbow up to further injury through rough kumi\-kata\. So I switched to righty for some of the matches\. I found ne\-waza easier and easier to guard my elbow\. I think it will be some time before I can do full randori with my normal kumi\-kata\.
