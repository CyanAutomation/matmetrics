# 2026-10-24 - Judo Session: Technical

## Techniques Practiced
- O\-soto\-gari
- O\-uchi\-gari
- Nami\-juji\-jime
- Gyaku\-juji\-jime
- Kata\-eri\-jime
- Turtle

## Session Description

We started with a normal warmup with commando crawls, ukemi, rolls, and similar\. Then we went into uchi\-komi, where I practiced o\-soto\-gari and o\-uchi\-gari\. We then revisited the techniques from last time, which include the belt\-loop turnover from turtle, which comprises threading a hand under the belt, then with the other hand grabbing the far shoulder and wrenching up the head, before getting into butterfly guard and pulling the opponent onto you, then flipping over for a turnover and hold down into tate\-shiho\-gatame\. The alternative is instead of threading the head arm to the far shoulder, just grabbing the available back of the neck lapel and pulling the opponent onto you, before proceeding with the flip and hold down\. Then we did a short session on cross\-collar chokes including nami\-juji\-jime, gyaku\-juji\-jime and kata\-eri\-jime\. Then we did a Pete special technique, which comprised passing from open guard, where you feed the bottom of the lapel around the thigh, pass it to the other hand under the thigh, then dropping your weight to pin the leg and squirm across the pinned leg to pass\. Lastly, we did a loop choke from butterfly guard, where you start with one hand in the lapel, then fall to one hip while looping the non\-choking arm around the head from the back\.

## Notes

It felt like a good session \- very technical and all techniques that I enjoyed\. I can see that a prior BJJ session on cross\-collar chokes has really helped make these techniques more effective and more natural\.
