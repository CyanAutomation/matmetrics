## Session Description

I missed the warm\-up today, but jumped straight into technique\. The teacher showed transitioning from closed guard to open guard with feet on the floor, then opening the partner's arms by shrimping to each side and holding them in this open guard\. From there, we worked on transitioning into spider guard and using the other leg to get a lasso\. We practiced unravelling the lasso, dropping the leg as a prop, and kicking the spider hook over to complete a sweep\. Lastly, we looked at moving from spider guard into a triangle, and practiced applying the sankaku\-jime\. We finished up with a small game trying to break and pass guard\.
