# 2024-08-19 - Judo Session: Shiai

## Techniques Practiced
- Ko\-soto\-gari
- Uchi\-mata
- Kesa\-gatame
- Harai\-goshi
- O\-soto\-gari

## Session Description

Today's Dan Grading session started with a match against Mr\. Hazelnut, someone I've faced before\. Our last competitive encounter saw me countering his aggression with a Kosoto\-gari for ippon\. This time, it felt similar, though he wasn't as aggressive\. We started with some grip setting and exchanging Kosoto\-gari jabs\. He went for a high arm grip, and I established my own grips\. When the timing felt right, I executed a two\-step Uchi\-mata, maintaining control through the throw for a solid ippon\. It was a quick, successful match that helped me warm up without expending too much energy\.

My next match was a tougher one against Mr\. Persimmon, who is quite strong and heavy\. From the start, I could feel his strength in the grips, but I think my grip fighting and movement were just slightly better\. After trading a few Kosoto\-gari attempts, I sensed his far leg was within reach\. I went for an O\-soto\-gari, and to my surprise, it worked, taking him down for a waza\-ari\. I immediately followed up with Kesa\-gatame, securing the pin for the required 10 seconds to seal the win\.

Winning both of those matches meant I was ready for my lineup\. The first match was against Mr\. Raspberry, who is younger and much lighter\. He came out strong, but the difference in strength and weight quickly became apparent\. He attempted a drop attack, which I countered easily, helping me settle into the match\. When the opportunity presented itself, my Uchi\-mata felt reliable, and I controlled him through the air for a full ippon\.

The second match in the lineup was against Mr\. Gooseberry, a young, strong, left\-handed player\. He started with a hopping Kosoto\-gari that unbalanced me a little, but it wasn't enough for a throw\. Then he managed to throw me with an O\-soto\-gari; I resisted, but eventually fell, giving him a waza\-ari\. We scrambled back to our feet, and he rushed at me, likely trying to capitalize on his momentum\. I sensed an opening, wrapped my arms around his neck and sleeve, and launched a Harai\-goshi\. He sailed over cleanly, landing flat on his back for ippon, bringing me back to win the match\.

My final match was with Mr\. Black Grape, a large and strong individual\. As soon as we started, I felt the immense strength in his grips\. He was powerful but didn't move around much\. By this point, I was feeling physically and mentally tired, and my judo began to slip\. I threw out an initial Kosoto\-gari that didn't land, and then a second\. He hadn't attacked yet, but he was clearly waiting to counter\. He caught my foot and threw me over with his version of Uchi\-mata, sending me straight onto my back\. And just like that, the match was over\.

## Notes

I completed the Dan grading event today at AWE Judo Club in Aldermaston, and overall, it was a very positive experience\. I had set my expectations to earn 20 points, but in the end, I managed to score 40 points\. The club was about 30 minutes away from home, and it turned out to be the busiest grading I’ve ever attended, with 37 participants\. This meant a lot of waiting around between matches\. Thankfully, I had the right nutrition for the day and stayed hydrated, fed, and warm between matches\.

In hindsight, the day was a success\. Watching the matches back, I can see that my judo was a mix of solid throws, too many non\-committal attacks, and some sloppy technique toward the end\. Although I wasn’t successful in the lineup, I managed to earn the remaining points needed to go for my black belt\.
