# 2024-03-09 - Judo Session: Shiai

## Techniques Practiced
- O\-uchi\-gari
- Uchi\-mata

## Session Description

My second match today was against a taller and heavier opponent\. The engagement started well, and my focus on grip fighting seemed to pay off, as I noticed he was getting frustrated by not securing good grips\. At one point, I attempted a sacrifice throw, which didn't quite land as intended, leading us to the ground\. He then tried to apply a strangle, but I managed to prevent it with defensive framing\. I think the combination of my defensive efforts and continued offense was wearing him down, as he started flagging quickly and making mistakes\. Eventually, after some grappling, I swept him with an O\-uchi\-gari straight for ippon\.

The third match felt like one I really should have done better in\. Before it started, I was consciously trying not to go too hard to avoid any risk of injury\. In the end, he managed to throw me with a drop technique, and I distinctly remember thinking as I went over, "I could spin out of this\." But I didn't\. The main positive takeaway from that encounter was successfully developing and testing a groundwork stalling technique, which proved quite effective\.

In one of the lineup matches, where I was second, I was able to secure a win\. Again, in this match, I felt my cardiovascular conditioning and defensive gripping really tired out my opponent\. I could clearly see he was visibly tired, panting, and starting to make errors\. I can't recall every technique from that exchange, but I do remember launching him with an Uchi\-mata for a half\-point score\.

## Notes

Today marks the completion of my second Dan grading event, this time at Bedford Judo Club\. Overall, it was a success—I won two matches and amassed 20 points—but somehow I feel it could’ve gone better\. 

I stepped on the scales below 81 kg, which was my goal, but I think it was evident that I was not as strong\. Possibly through the kilograms I have lost, I have also lost muscle mass\. It’s hard to describe, but I just felt a little bit weaker today\. On the positive side, I felt like I had more cardiovascular health, which actually served me well\.
