# 2024-05-05 - Judo Session: Shiai

## Techniques Practiced
- Uchi\-mata
- Ko\-soto\-gari

## Session Description

Today's session involved several matches\. My first match was against Mr\. Gourd\. He was taller, heavier, and stronger, but I noticed he was slower than me\. We had a lot of equal exchanges, but I felt I could win\. I focused on maintaining pressure with my gripping and constant foot techniques, which eventually led to a successful ko\-soto\-gari sweep, scoring a full point\. I felt my approach of solid gripping and continuous foot technique pressure really paid off in this one\.My second match was against Mr\. Melon\. He was my size but heavier, stronger, and, importantly, left\-handed\. It took a few exchanges for me to realize his preference\. He had strong gripping and defended many of my attacks effectively\. I managed to defend a pinning attempt by transitioning into half\-guard\. For most of the match, he was ahead, but in the last minute, he started to tire\. I capitalized on this with a rolling uchi mata\. Honestly, I didn't expect it to work and was quite surprised when it did\.Next up was Mr\. Asparagus, who was tall, lanky, and strong\. He was very good at defending his lapel, making it tough for me to establish a good grip\. He took an early lead, and I felt the match starting to slip away\. However, I kept applying pressure with my foot techniques, and I could sense him fatiguing\. With only seconds left, he attempted a sloppy uchi mata, resulting in his head hitting the floor and an immediate disqualification\. Both of us seemed surprised by the outcome, but I was grateful for the win\.Finally, after just a short three\-minute break, I faced Mr\. Polish Radish\. He was composed, strong, and very focused\. After a brief exchange of grips, he quickly dropped into his trademark drop seoi nage, throwing me over for a full score, and the match was over\.

## Notes

Today was my first experience at the 2024 BJC Nationals competition in Kettering\. Overall, it was a success\. However, it’s curious how my emotional state fluctuates so much leading up to the first match\. I made sure to eat a proper meal before the warm\-up and did the right amount of warm\-up\. It's crazy how nervous and anxious I was, and it goes to show that no matter how impressive the other judoka look in the warm\-up area, I must not let it affect me or how I feel about my matches\.

Overall, it was a good outcome and a success, with some good fortune on my side\. Today, as with the last competition, strong gripping and stamina served me well\. I think the focus on consistent foot technique pressure also paid off, and this is something I should look to refine even further\. Going forward, in Randori, I should continue to refine diverse foot techniques, as well as drop seoi nage defense\.
