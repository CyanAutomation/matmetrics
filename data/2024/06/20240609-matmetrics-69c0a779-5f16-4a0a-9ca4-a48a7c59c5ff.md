# 2024-06-09 - Judo Session: Shiai

## Techniques Practiced
- Tomoe\-nage
- O\-uchi\-gari
- Uchi\-mata
- O\-soto\-gari
- Hiza\-guruma

## Session Description

Today's session involved a few good matches, some more challenging than others\.My first match was against Mr\. Persimon\. He was an older guy, roughly my size, and seemed to have a good understanding of grips, but wasn't really attacking with them\. At one point, he managed to get a strong grip, and I tried to escape with a tomoe nage, which didn't quite work out, leading to a stall on the ground\. Once we were back on our feet, I got back to attacking\. I managed to hit my go\-to combination, an O\-uchi gari straight into an Uchi\-mata, which scored the full 10 points for the win\.Match two was against Mr\. Lemongrass, who was taller and seemed to prefer waiting to counter rather than initiating attacks\. I kept pushing forward with my own attacks\. After a few exchanges, I went for an O\-soto gari that didn't quite land perfectly, earning me a half score\. Immediately after, I transitioned out of his guard position into a pin\. He tried to roll away, but I managed to catch his bicep, moved into an armbar position, and eventually pivoted into a solid hold\-down\. Holding him for 10 seconds was enough to secure that win\.After a long wait, it was time for my batsugun\. Everyone in my lineup was new to me, which definitely added an extra layer of challenge\. My first fight was against an opponent who was aggressive but also a bit sloppy, especially on the ground\. We ended up in ground exchanges multiple times, but I just couldn't capitalize with a choke or a pin\. It was frustrating because I felt comfortable and confident on the ground, but I wasn't decisive or skilled enough to finish\. After a few resets, I tried a Hiza guruma, and to my surprise, it actually worked, earning me the full 10 points\.Without any break, I went straight into my second batsugun match\. I engaged, got my grips, and then tried a somewhat sloppy O\-uchi gari\. My opponent countered it really well, landing me flat on my back for a full and definitive loss\. That was pretty disappointing\. I think if I hadn't been so tired, I probably wouldn't have been so sloppy, and the match could have gone differently\. This is the second time I've been caught by a Tsubame gaeshi counter, so it's clear I really need to develop a solid defense against that\.

This was a milestone event, being my first judo batsugun \(lineup challenge\)\. Overall, it was a positive grading with 30 points, although I would’ve liked to reach the last match of the lineup\. In hindsight, fitness was good, weight was good, and mindset was good\. So, overall, it was a positive result for the day\.
