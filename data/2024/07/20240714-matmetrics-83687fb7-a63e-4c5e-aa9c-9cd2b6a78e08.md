## Session Description

Today's randori session had some interesting matchups\. My first opponent, Mr\. Pinecone, was taller with a longer reach, which made it tough\. He seemed a bit uncomfortable with my left\-hand attacks, but he quickly adapted and managed to establish a strong high grip\. The match went on for a while, but eventually, I was thrown\.My second match was against Mr\. Hazelnut, who I think I might've competed against before\. He came out very aggressively, but his attacks felt a bit sloppy\. I tried to stay patient and wait for an opening\. When he committed to a sweep, I was able to sidestep and counter with a kosoto gari, throwing him onto his back for an ippon\. That felt good to execute cleanly\.My third match, against Mr\. Mushroom, was the most frustrating\. He consistently went for tomoe nage\. The first time he attempted it, I managed to defend successfully\. However, when we re\-engaged, he got much better grips on me\. With those dominant grips, he executed the tomoe nage again, throwing me for an ippon\. It's really frustrating because I know that if I hadn't allowed him to establish those advantageous grips, he wouldn't have been able to throw me\. It's a clear reminder that grip fighting is crucial, especially against a persistent tomoe nage user\.
